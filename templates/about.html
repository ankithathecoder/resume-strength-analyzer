Resume Analyzer

About This Project

Resume Strength Analyzer is a machine learning–based system designed to evaluate resumes and classify their overall strength.

Why this system?

Many resumes get rejected due to poor structure or lack of clarity. This system helps users understand how strong their resume is.

Limitations

The system assumes that the resume is relevant to the job role and does not check domain relevance.

Future Scope
  • Job-role specific scoring
  • Section-wise resume feedback
  • Advanced NLP models
